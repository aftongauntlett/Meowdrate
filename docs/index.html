A hydration app that starts full instead of empty.

How it works

Three steps, no learning curve, no meter to grind.

1

Log a drink

Tap in, take a few real sips while a short timer runs, then confirm. No manual ounce math.

2

The flood recedes

Each logged drink pulls the water down a little. The sky keeps shifting with real time, whether you're keeping up or not.

3

Cat rescued

Clear the flood fully and the cat's safe for the day — with a dry comment from the narrator to match.
